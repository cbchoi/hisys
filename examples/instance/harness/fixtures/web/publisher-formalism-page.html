Dynamic Structure DEVS and Self-Organizing Structure Formalisms

doi:10.0000/hisys.fixture.formalism

Dynamic Structure DEVS supports executable model structures whose topology can change during simulation. Graph rewriting captures local structural transformation rules, while agent-based modeling captures decentralized emergence. The literature still lacks a unified account that combines local rewrite rules, feedback, topology/behavior co-evolution, executable simulation semantics, and analyzable structural constraints for self-organizing systems.
